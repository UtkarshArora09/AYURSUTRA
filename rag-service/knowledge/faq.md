# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment through the AyurSutra app by selecting a doctor,
choosing an available time slot from the dynamic queue system, and confirming
your booking. You will receive a confirmation and reminder via the app.

## What is the dynamic queue system?
AyurSutra uses a self-correcting queue that estimates wait times based on a
default 5-minute consultation slot, adjusting in real time if a doctor runs
over or under the estimated time, so patients get more accurate wait estimates.

## Can I have a video consultation instead of visiting in person?
Yes. AyurSutra supports WebRTC-based video consultations. You can join a video
call with your doctor directly through the app at your scheduled time.

## What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring any previous medical records, a list of current medications, and
arrive 10 minutes early to complete your patient profile if you haven't already.

## How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
Go to "My Appointments" in the app, select the appointment, and choose
reschedule or cancel. Please do this at least 2 hours in advance when possible.
