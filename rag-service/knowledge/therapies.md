# Panchakarma Therapies at AyurSutra

## Vamana
Vamana is a therapeutic emesis (vomiting) procedure used primarily to eliminate
excess Kapha dosha from the body. It is recommended for conditions like chronic
asthma, allergies, and skin disorders. The procedure is done under strict
supervision after a preparatory phase of oleation (Snehapana) and fomentation
(Swedana). Duration: typically a single-day procedure preceded by 3-7 days of prep.

## Virechana
Virechana is a purgation therapy aimed at eliminating excess Pitta dosha. It is
commonly used for skin conditions, chronic fever, and digestive disorders. Like
Vamana, it requires preparatory oleation and fomentation before the main procedure.

## Basti
Basti (medicated enema) is considered the most important Panchakarma procedure,
especially effective for Vata-related disorders such as arthritis, constipation,
and neurological conditions. There are two main types: Anuvasana Basti (oil-based)
and Niruha Basti (decoction-based). A full Basti course can range from 8 to 30 days
depending on the condition.

## Nasya
Nasya involves administration of medicated oils or powders through the nostrils.
It is used for disorders of the head and neck region, including sinusitis,
migraines, and certain neurological conditions. Sessions are typically short,
around 15-30 minutes, and may be repeated over several days.

## Raktamokshana
Raktamokshana is a blood-letting procedure used to treat blood-borne disorders
and certain skin conditions. It is used less frequently than the other four
procedures and only when specifically indicated.
